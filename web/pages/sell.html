FAQ

Who can apply?

Any signed-in user without an existing shop row can submit a pending application.

When does my store go public?

Only shops with status approved appear in the directory and on product cards.

Can I change my status myself?

No. Approval and rejection are admin actions. You can update your own shop details after approval rules allow it.
